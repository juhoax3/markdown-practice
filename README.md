## Olgierd von Everec - The Witcher 3: Hearts of Stone

### *Iris (Sword)*

**Geralt** can obtain this sword at the end of the DLC if the player makes the right choices.

Iris is a unique steel sword associated with Olgierd von Everec. ***It has a special ability that charges the blade during combat.***

***The sword can unleash a powerful strike when the blade glows red.***

### Sword Features

- **Weapon type:** Steel sword
- Rarity: Relic
- Special ability: Charges energy during combat
- Expansion: Hearts of Stone

### How to Obtain Iris

1. Play through the *Hearts of Stone* expansion.
2. Reach the final quest, `Whatsoever a Man Soweth...`
3. Make the choices that allow Geralt to obtain the sword.

For more information, visit the [Olgierd von Everec Wiki](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Olgierd_von_Everec).
